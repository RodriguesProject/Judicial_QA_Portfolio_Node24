# Recruiter Walkthrough — 3 Minutes

1. Open `README.md` to understand scope and technology.
2. Open `docs/01_requirements.md` and `test-data/test-cases.csv` to see traceability.
3. Open `tests/` to inspect executable QA tests.
4. Open `tests/test_security.py` for authorization testing.
5. Open `tests/test_integration.py` for audit/data-integrity testing.
6. Open `tests/test_e2e.py` for an end-to-end workflow.
7. Open `reports/test-summary-report.md` for execution results and limitations.
8. Open `.github/workflows/qa.yml` to see CI automation.
